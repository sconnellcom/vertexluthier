Made to travel

Designed and built from scratch by Vertex Luthier Noah Connell, this 3D-printed electric cello combines a minimalist body with a lightweight carbon fiber endpin for a compact, performance-ready instrument.

Packs down to carry-on size

The entire cello breaks down into the included hard-shell case, measuring 22 × 13.2 × 8 inches (56 × 33.5 × 20 cm). Check your airline's current carry-on limits before travelling.

Built to protect

The case has a hard aluminum alloy exterior and an impact-absorbing foam-lined interior to cushion your instrument in transit.

Assembles in minutes

Plug in and play
